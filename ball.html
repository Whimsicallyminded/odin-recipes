Protein Balls

Quick,easy,and healthy oatmeal protein balls!

I like to have a batch of these in my refrigerator every week. Not only do they taste great, but they are so easy to make! Most of us have the majority of the needed ingredients already in our pantry. So go gather them up and let's get to it!

Ingreditents

  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1/3 cup honey
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup rolled oats
  • 1/2 cup protein powder
  • 1/4 cup chia seeds
  • 1/4 cup dark chocolate chips
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • pink of sea Salt

Steps

  1. Mix wet ingredients
  2. Add dry ingredients and mix well
  3. Fold in chocolate chips
  4. Roll into 1-inch balls
  5. Place them on parchment-lined baking sheet and refrigerate for 30 minutes
  6. Keep in airtight container in fridge for up to one week!
