Protein Brownie

Who says brownies can't be healthy?

When it comes to diet it sometimes seems like we have to sacrifice some of the yummiest treats! so we decided to take one of everyones favorite treat and add protein. it's not complicated or fancy, but it tastes great and helps reach those protein goals for the day!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup of almond flour
  • 1/3 cup protein powder
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/3 cup melted coconut oil or butter
  • 2 eggs
  • 2/3 cup sugar
  • 1 tsp pure vanilla
  • 1/2 cup mini chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 350 F
  2. Mix all ingredients in mixing bowl until well blended
  3. Grease 8-inch pan and pour mixture evenly into greased pan
  4. Bake 20 minutes
  5. Allow to cool and they are ready to serve!
